How this works

MonTueWed

One a day. Each workout gives the Tank row a Tank I. Steps, health logging and cardio fill a bank that turns into Ranger, Health cat and Rogue cats.

→

Three make one. On the Ranger, Health cat and Rogue rows, 3 of a tier make 1 of the next. The Tank row costs 5 · 4 · 5 · 3 · 3.

gap−1

A gap costs the smallest one. When a bank runs down the row loses its smallest cat, and a big one breaks back into its parts rather than vanishing.

no cost

Your own rest days are free. The Tank row's allowance comes from your schedule, and a rest or deload day the app asked for never counts against it.

Why. The row is the rule: you can read "5 Tank I make a Tank II" straight off the chain, and empty rungs show how far the ladder goes. Four rows fit about one screen with every roster closed (Steps is shown open). Rule numbers keep coming from the engine, so the page can't drift from it.

Optional: backdrops from titles (#2189)

The titles are built: forest for your first Ranger IV, house for your first Health cat III, castle for your first Tank IV, read from the best tier each row has ever reached. This card is where you would pick one. It is shown so you can say yes or no to it with the screen. It can also come later.
