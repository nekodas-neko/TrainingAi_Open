Home screen · score row · design exploration

Twenty-eight ways to draw four numbers — without touching the icons.

The four metric buttons at the top of Home are the most-looked-at, least-designed surface in the app. Every mockup below keeps the Lucide glyph and its identity colour exactly as they are today — blue bolt, red pulse, purple moon, orange flame. Everything around them is up for grabs: the frame, the fill, the shape, the layout, whether there's a frame at all.

Three things worth deciding before any of these ship

1. Heart Rate has no percentage. 52 bpm is not 52% of anything, so every fill-based design needs a defined arc length for it. The mockups derive it from the existing restingHrCue tiers (Low / Steady / Elevated / High → 85 / 70 / 45 / 25), which keeps the arc honest but means the HR ring encodes a tier, not the number under it. The alternative is a frame-only treatment for HR while the other three fill — visually inconsistent, but never misleading.

2. Colour-only state is a rule violation. CLAUDE.md forbids conveying state by colour alone. Anything that drops today's dot needs the band word, a symbol, or a fill length carrying the same information — the aria-label alone is not enough for a sighted user. Designs tagged encodes value satisfy this through arc length; designs tagged needs a state cue do not yet.

3. Row height is a budget. Today's row costs ~140dp of the first screen. The compact layouts at the end buy back 60–90dp for the recommendation card, which is the thing you actually act on.
