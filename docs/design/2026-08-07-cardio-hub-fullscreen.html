The one thing to decide about C1

Cyan is the Start Workout colour. In the C1 screen above there are now two cyan elements stacked ~70dp apart, and the outlined one sits lower — closer to the thumb, which on a 6.9" screen is the more reachable position. The risk isn't that it looks bad; it's that the eye lands on the wrong action first.

Two ways out if that bothers you on device: give Cardio Hub the walk modality's green (#22c55e, already in use in the hub's own picker), or keep the cyan but drop the border to 1px and the label to regular weight so it reads as clearly secondary. The mockup uses 1.5px and bold, which is the most aggressive version.

What I could not check

These are computed layouts in Chromium, not the APK. Safe-area is the specific unknown: the nav bar here reserves 22dp of bottom inset, but on Android gesture-nav env(safe-area-inset-bottom) often reports near zero, which is why this repo uses floored utilities. Neither variant is bottom-anchored — both sit above the nav — so neither is at risk, but the real clearance under the row is only visible on the device.
