What I need from you

1. D as the wallpaper-on look, with a fallback. I’d pair D1 (or D2 if you want the water inside) for when the dynamic wallpaper is on with R1 · Tide from round 2 for when it’s off. Both are the same dial gauge with a different surface, so it’s one component with a switch, not two.

2. The dot is the sun. It borrows the colour for the time of day from the wallpaper’s own palette. If you’d rather it never change colour, “White dot” is the same thing without the link.

3. Glass tiles for the pillars (what D1 uses) or glass circles in your ring style? I’d offer glass as the wallpaper-on treatment of whichever ring style you’ve chosen.

4. Height. Full D is a little taller than the Tide (about 430 dp), so the same three-size setting applies: Full, Compact (D3), Slim (D4).

5. Weather. Rain, cloud and snow already change the wallpaper. I’d leave the dial alone in all of them. Only the sky moves.

Still open from earlier: big number live or fixed, a projected line, whether Energy can rise after a nap, the rename scope, and the weights (#2826, #2833).

What I did not exercise: a real wallpaper on the phone (these skies are approximations built from the app’s palette values, without its clouds, particles or sun position), backdrop blur on the Samsung WebView, light mode on the device, real safe-area, and the cost of the sky crossfade.
