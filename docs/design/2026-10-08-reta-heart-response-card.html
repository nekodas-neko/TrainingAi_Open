What you're deciding

  • Placement: a section in the vial sheet, under Weight response. That's where the dose lives, and it's one sheet you already open. The alternative is its own screen, which has more room but is one more place to go.
  • "Clear pattern": it's shown only when, on at least one day, the middle half of your doses sits fully outside your pre-reta normal (resting HR 50–52, HRV 53–65). Otherwise it says "No clear pattern yet" and gives no sentence. Today it qualifies: on day 4, resting HR's middle half is 58–63 against a normal of 50–52. It never says reta caused it.
  • Dose 1's time: 7 Sep has no time logged because the field didn't exist yet. The card lines doses up by day, so it isn't needed. If you remember roughly when you took it, edit that dose in the app. Otherwise leave it.

One thing the numbers show that the card says plainly: on 1 mg your resting HR hasn't come back to normal by the next dose (still about +6 on day 6), so each dose starts from a raised baseline.
