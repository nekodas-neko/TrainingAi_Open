How the two earlier decisions fit together

  • They point different ways. TN-67 says ask on unusual days. OR-171 and TN-82 say don't ask: state what the app filled in, and let you correct it. The sleep check-in works that way now, because every question on that sheet stopped being answered (0 touched answers in 102 check-ins).
  • The recommendation uses both. Usual days follow TN-82: a statement, no question. Unusual days follow TN-67: one question, on roughly the two days a week you agreed to. It reuses the sleep check-in's quiet and prominent shapes rather than adding a new one.
  • What it can't show. Asking only on unusual days never samples an ordinary-looking day you would have called bad. So the answers tell us whether the unusual days felt unusual, not whether the score is right overall. Nothing built on them will be described as validating the score.

What to reply

"Yes" builds the recommended pair (quiet line, question on unusual days). "Hide it" builds the alternative. Or say what to change, on #2106.
