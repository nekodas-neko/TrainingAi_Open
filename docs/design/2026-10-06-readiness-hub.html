Readiness as the master pillar

Readiness becomes one hub built from four pillars: Sleep, Heart, Activity, Body. It also absorbs Body Battery. The ring's morning mark is today's readiness, and the filled arc is the energy you have left, which drains and recharges through the day. Each pillar tile shows its score, its share of the blend and one line saying why.

What sits under each pillar

Temperature moves out of Heart and into the new Body pillar. Tapping a tile opens that pillar's own screen, which lists these inputs.

Sleep · 82

  • Last night
  • Sleep balance
  • Consistency

Heart · 70

  • Resting HR
  • HRV
  • Overnight recovery

Activity · 65

  • Yesterday's movement
  • Activity balance
  • Training load: ACWR 7:28, plus the 28:90 block trend

Body · 74 new

  • Temperature deviation
  • Respiratory rate
  • SpO₂
  • Weight and body-composition trend
  • Daytime stress
  • Fuel: energy balance vs budget, protein, hydration

How the number is made

Readiness is a weighted blend of the four pillar scores. Each tile prints its weight and its contribution, so the sum can be checked on screen.

PillarScoreWeightAdds
Sleep8230%24.6
Heart7030%21.0
Activity6520%13.0
Body7420%14.8
Readiness100%73.4 → 73

The weights are a tuning decision and need your sign-off. 30 / 30 / 20 / 20 is only an example. They will ship as a type: tuning proposal that says how many past days move and by how much.

History would be re-scored. Past readiness is recomputed from stored pillar inputs under the new blend, so trends compare like with like. Days missing a pillar (for example, no SpO₂ before the ring supported it) blend over the pillars they have, and the tile says so.

Body Battery keeps its engine. It still opens at readiness and drains with HR load and stress. Only the presentation merges, so the morning mark and the fill can never disagree.

Reversal cost is low for the layout: the hub replaces one card and one chip. Re-scoring history is the costly part, so the weights should be settled before it runs.
