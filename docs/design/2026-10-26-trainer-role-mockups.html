Recommendation: A. It puts coaching beside the thing you actually do, which is build and send programs. Someone who trains three people finds all of them in one place a year from now, and nothing new appears in the bottom navigation.

B is better at reusing the friend-request habit and the friend list. It loses because training someone is not a social act, and the list would mix the two.

C is better at being discoverable from a person. It loses because there is no overview of who you train, and a trainee with no friend profile has no home.
