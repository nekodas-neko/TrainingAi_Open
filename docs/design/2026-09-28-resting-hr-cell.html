What is free: the caption slot under the number. The component already draws one there for the cue word at 7.5 px, and in the default style that slot renders nothing. A bpm caption at ~9 px costs about 22 px under a 46 px number.

The fork — and it exists because there are nineteen ring styles

SCORE_RING_STYLES is user-selectable and you have picked between nineteen of them. nolabel removes the label on purpose — the glyph is the name — so a unit caption there fights the premise of the style, and overlap has no caption slot at all. No single treatment is right for all nineteen. That is the whole question.

A — a bpm caption under the number, recommended. One word, in a slot that renders nothing in the default style, reversible in one component. It does not re-open a visual language you have already chosen between nineteen times.

B — drop the ring on the HR cell. The honest version of "it is not a score": a ring encodes 0–100 progress, and 58 bpm has no such scale. It fixes every one of the nineteen styles at once, including the ones A cannot reach.

Where A deliberately does nothing, and it is the case against it. You can choose nolabel, which removes the label because the glyph is the name, and overlap, which has no caption slot. In those two, A changes nothing and 58 still reads as a score with nothing naming the metric at all — which is where the ambiguity is worst. If you use either style, pick B.

Not device-verified. Drawn from the measured geometry, not screenshotted on the S25 — the row needs live scores. Whichever wins owes a device look, especially B, which changes a shape rather than adding a word.

What I need: A or B. If you regularly use the nolabel or overlap ring style, say so — that alone decides it for B.

Reversal cost is low either way: one component, no stored data.
