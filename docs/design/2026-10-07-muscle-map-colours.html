Muscle Volume: one colour scale for the map and the bars

For #2554. Today the map shades every trained muscle green, from dark (under 20% of target) to light (at target). The bars below show the same muscles in red. Proposal: the map uses the bars' own rule. Green means at target, amber 60–99%, red under 60%, and grey means untrained. The example uses your week from the screenshot.

  • The map and the bars read from one function (components/health/weekly-muscle-sets-card.tsx's colour rule moves to a shared helper that MuscleHeatmap also uses). Two rules for one number would be a bug.
  • Red, amber and green are the app's tokens (--destructive, --accent-amber, --accent-green). Each must keep 3:1 contrast against the untrained grey, as the current test (RV-101) requires.
  • Injured muscles keep their own red outline marker, so injury is not confused with "under target".
