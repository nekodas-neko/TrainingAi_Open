Reworked screens

NUTRITION, REWORKED — six screens at true S25 size (412 dp) in the app's own tokens: Geist, oklch(0.05) page, oklch(0.09) cards, 10 and 14 px radii, the real MACRO_COLORS trio. The conventions taken from mainstream food loggers are structural, not visual — a list row that never carries a control, a grey second line saying what and how much, the calorie number right-aligned in the same column on every screen, and a macro donut showing each macro as a share of calories. The chrome, colour and type are ours throughout; nothing here copies another app's look. The single biggest change is that editing moved off the row and into a sheet. That is what makes everything else fit.
ON PHOTOS The thumbnails are placeholders — a tile and a glyph, not a bad attempt at food photography. Storing them is a real decision, not a detail: a data-URI thumbnail rides in the sync delta and the on-device SQLite mirror, once per meal, forever. The size cap is the whole design. See Q-396.
WHY EVERY ROW LOOKS THE SAME Today a food reads one way in the diary, another in search, another in a saved meal, and another again in the meal builder. Four shapes for one thing. Here there is one: optional thumbnail · name · grey line of what and how much · calories, right-aligned in a fixed column · optional chevron. It is used on all six screens above, which is what makes the whole section feel like one app rather than six sheets that grew separately. That also makes it a component — `components/nutrition/food-row.tsx` — rather than markup repeated at six call sites, which is the shape the repo's own reuse rule asks for.

The review

THE REVIEW THAT CAME FIRST — kept because the findings still bind whatever gets built. Every control on the shipped screens is 44 px against this repo's own 48 dp rule, and the srv/g segments are 40. The hardcoded green ignores both the accent the user picks and the darkening light mode applies. The three srv/g options are superseded by the sheet on page one — the toggle now appears once, at 56 px, instead of on every row — but the reasoning is here rather than lost.
