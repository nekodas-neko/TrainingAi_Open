What I need from you

1. Which one? I would take R1 · Tide. It is A with E’s liquid inside, it keeps the exact ring and notch, and it answers “a gauge around it that drops”. R3 is the cheap version if you would rather have only the liquid.

2. Hero size as a setting. R1 is tall (see the bars above). Since you already choose ring styles in Settings, the hero could have three sizes you pick: Full (R1), Compact (R4) and Slim (R8). That keeps the big Energy moment for people who want it, and gives the screen back to the others.

3. Pillars. Plain circles (as in A) or mini tanks? I would keep circles on the default and offer tanks as a ring-style choice, because five liquids at once competes with the hero.

4. Water motion. Still water that lowers is the safe first build. The sideways drift and any sloshing are extras to test on the phone first, and I have not tested any motion on the phone.

5. Water colour. I kept the accent. An amber or red tank when low is easy to add; the label already carries the state.

6. Water tied to stress? The choppy-water idea is fun, but daytime stress is not validated yet (Q-507, TN-33), so I would not draw it on the screen as a fact.

Still open from round 1: whether the big number is live or fixed (drawn live here), the projected line, whether Energy can go up after a nap, the rename scope, and the weights (#2826, #2833).

What I did not exercise: real device rendering (Samsung WebView, safe-area, the S25’s real width), motion cost on the phone, the white number against bright accents on a real screen, the native ScoreChip, and live data.
