#2833 recommends keeping it inside Body until two scores have steady daily data. Meal times are also unreliable until #2829.

What I need from you

1. Which hero? I would take A · Crown for Home and G for the detail screen. Crown is about 270 dp taller than today’s score row (about 225 without the signals strip), measured in the page; B, C, D and E are 280–290 dp taller and G is 440. That is a real cost on a daily-use screen, so the signals strip should be optional and the ring could shrink to 176 px. If you want something with more drama, D (dial over the sky) is the one worth a second round, as it only works with the wallpaper on.

2. Does the big number move? In these panes the big number is live (46 at 15:00) with the morning mark kept as a notch and “woke at 76”. The other reading is a fixed 76 with only the arc dropping. I prefer live: a gauge that drops beside a number that does not looks broken, and the stored morning value is what trends and history use either way.

3. A projected line? The dashed rest-of-day line on the detail screen is an estimate. I would leave it out of the first build and add it when there are enough days to check it against.

4. Can it go up? Today Body Battery only drains (TN-15 says recharge is missing). A nap or a long quiet hour could add a few points, and the arc would need an “up” state. I drew it down-only.

5. Naming. “Readiness” appears in about 400 files and in copy like the deload and session-fit text. I would rename only what people read, and leave the internal names (tables, routes, readinessDisplayScore) alone so no migration is needed.

Weights are still yours: 30 / 30 / 20 / 20 is the example from 06 Oct, and #2826 / #2833 hold the real call, including letting the pillar totals float. History would be re-scored under whatever you pick.

What I did not exercise: real device rendering (Samsung WebView, safe-area, the S25’s real 412 dp width), a real wallpaper behind D, the native ScoreChip implementation, and live data. Everything here is drawn from the app’s theme tokens at 384 px, not run in the app.
