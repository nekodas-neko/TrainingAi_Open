**Headline results:**

1. **The AI periodization engine's rep-completion signal chain is permanently dead** (AI-1,
   high): `setLastSessionRanPrescription` is never called anywhere, so `repCompletionRate` is
   always null — autoregulation back-off always takes the mildest cut, rep pushes fire on RPE
   alone, and the emergency-deload `< 0.7` trigger can never fire.
2. **Prescriptions are stale by design** (AI-2, high): generated only at the *previous*
   session's completion and consumed up to 7 days later with no re-evaluation — today's
   soreness/readiness/morning check-in never influence today's weights; per-exercise soreness
   deloads live 7 days despite the whole-session variant deliberately expiring in 1.
3. **Supersets silently lose their rest alerts** (TMR-1, high): the superset handoff nulls or
   stales the live rest anchor, so the rest beep and OS notification never fire during
   alternation rests. This also **corrects R4 WK-3's premise** (the beep/notification are NOT
   fine — they get the right duration but a dead/stale start).
4. **APK data-visibility holes remain after R3 Chunk 1** (SYN-1/SYN-2, high): the Stats tab's
   workout edit/delete has no local-store mirror (R3's shipped Task 1.2 fixed only
   health-content despite citing stats-content in scope), and whole-session deletes never touch
   the local store — deleted sessions keep rendering on the deleting device.
5. **AI-prescription accept/dismiss never invalidates the `workout-card:` freshWithinTtl key**
   (CCH-1, high): home/workout-select serve the pre-prescription card payload for up to 6 h.

## 2. AI dynamic system, phases, weight progression (AI)

**Architecture (how the dynamic system actually works):** `ai_dynamic` programs give each `program_sessions` row a `session_periodization` state row (phase, sessions_in_phase, baseline1rm JSONB, prescription JSONB + status/expiry). Baseline phase serves 1 AMRAP set/exercise; completion is detected by the GET-state route's auto-heal (PR-anchored) → accumulation. On every workout completion, `complete-workout` marks the current prescription `consumed`, increments `sessions_in_phase`, then fire-and-forgets `POST /prescribe` for the *next* session of that type. `prescribe` aggregates ~30 signals (PR-based 1RM trends, plateau projection, RPE deltas, soreness/injuries, ACWR, sleep/HRV/SpO2 trends, Oura readiness, morning check-in, weekly volume vs MEV/MAV/MRV, deterministic confidence). Deterministic pre-passes short-circuit the LLM (emergency whole-session deload; per-exercise soreness deload). Otherwise Gemini (`generateObject` + schema, `withAiRetry`) returns phase/phase_action/per-exercise sets-reps-pct-rest; `reconcilePrescription` normalizes pct fractions, drops hallucinated ids, backfills omissions. Post-passes: RPE×1RM autoregulation, `clampPrescribedPct`, `fitToBudget`, phase ceilings forcing `transition_recommended`. Stored with 7-day expiry, status `pending`; auto-applies only at deterministic confidence ≥0.6 + opt-in + `stay`. Consumption: `workout-data` expands the prescription into a synthetic per-set style; the workout screen converts to kg via `lastLog.estimated1rm × pct` rounded to the equipment step; primaries get an AMRAP last set, others +1 rep — beating targets raises the next 1RM (prescriptionFactor makes an exact hit 1RM-neutral: this is the entire weight-progression engine). Logging recomputes 1RM server-side and updates `personal_records` through `shouldCountTowardPr`.

**AI-1 | high | NEW | lib/data/postgres/slices/periodization.ts:178, lib/ai-periodization/signals.ts:250** — `setLastSessionRanPrescription` is never called — the rep-completion signal chain is permanently dead. The writer exists in the slice, adapter (adapter.ts:4266) and repository interface (repository.ts:572), but no route or workflow ever invokes it, so `state.lastSessionRanPrescription` is always null and the gate at signals.ts:250 never opens. Consequences: `repCompletionRate` and `perExCompletion` are always null → autoregulation's back-off is always sized at the mildest 5% cut (autoregulation.ts:57), the push gate `(repCompletionRate ?? 1) >= 1` (autoregulation.ts:69) treats "no data" as "reps met" so rep pushes fire on RPE alone, the emergency-deload trigger `repCompletionRate < 0.7` (emergency-deload.ts:27) can never fire, and the prompt always prints "Rep completion rate: no data" (prompt.ts:207). Fix: set the flag in `completeWorkoutFromPayload` (complete-workout.ts:39-42) from the completed session's prescription status.

**AI-2 | high | NEW | app/api/complete-workout/route.ts:44, app/api/workout-data/route.ts:244-246** — Prescriptions are generated only at the *previous* session's completion and served up to 7 days later with no expiry/staleness check at the consumption point. Every recovery signal (soreness, HRV/sleep/SpO2 trends, external readiness, morning check-in, weekly volume) is captured minutes after the *prior* workout — today's morning check-in and soreness never influence the numbers that reach the bar. Expiry is enforced only as a side effect of the GET-state route (:50-57) and only flips *pending*→dismissed; the load path applies `state.prescription` via `prescriptionDrivesLoad` with no expiry or generated-at check — an `accepted` prescription drives load indefinitely. Worst sub-case: per-exercise soreness deloads are baked in with the full 7-day life even though the whole-session soreness variant deliberately expires in 1 day (prescribe route :184). Fix: re-run the deterministic layers (per-exercise deload + autoregulation, on fresh signals) when the prescription is first consumed on a different local day than it was generated.

**AI-3 | med-high | NEW | lib/ai-periodization/emergency-deload.ts:22-23, lib/ai-periodization/signals.ts:196-211** — Emergency-deload conditions are evaluated at generation time, when their semantics are inverted: `hoursSinceLastSession` is ≈0 in every evaluation (prescribe only runs right after completion), so `< 36 && soreMuscles >= 3` degenerates to "was sore on completion day"; `consecutiveSessionDaysOfThisType` counts the just-trained day, biasing the `>= 4` trigger. **AI-4 | medium | NEW | lib/ai-periodization/emergency-deload.ts:24 vs lib/ai-periodization/prompt.ts:105-109** — Any active in-session injury deterministically forces an emergency whole-session deload (prescribe route :143-160) before the AI runs, making the prompt's documented injury path (`session_swap_recommended` + reduced load on overlapping exercises) unreachable.
