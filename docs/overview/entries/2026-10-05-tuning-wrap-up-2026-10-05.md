## TN-86 — the finding that was still only prose

`TN-84` records a deviation made while building `TN-85`: the Home note cannot say *"90 min later than
usual"*, because `sleep_verdicts` snapshots each band's `low`/`high` and **drops
`ComponentBand.median`**. It ships *"65 min later than usual"* measured to the band edge instead.

That gap lived only inside `TN-84`'s body, and `TN-84` is `Lane: O` — an owner copy decision — so the
buildable half was invisible to Lane A. Under **No orphaned findings** that is a dropped finding, and
the split-it-out rule says a buildable half buried in another lane's entry gets its own entry. Filed as
**`TN-86`, `Lane: A`**: three nullable columns plus a migration and its `claude_ro` twin.

Two things stated plainly in it, because conflating them makes this look like a copy problem:

- **The shipped copy is arguably better.** "65 min past the late end of your usual range" is a smaller,
  truer claim than "90 min later than usual", and it is the quantity the verdict acted on. The entry does
  not ask to reverse that.
- **The snapshot is incomplete regardless of the wording**, which is the actual cost. The design's
  load-bearing claim is that *a correction whose paired verdict is not pinned is not evidence*. The median
  is part of what the verdict judged against, so without it a correction can never be asked **how far from
  centre** the night sat — only which side of a threshold it fell. It cannot be back-filled either,
  because the trailing window has moved. Not urgent; does not improve by waiting.

Also recorded there: **do not bump `SLEEP_VERDICT_MODEL_VERSION` for it.** Adding a recorded field changes
nothing about the rule that produced a verdict, and the version exists to pair a correction with the rule
it disagreed with.
